## 🎯 Anchor Text Distribution Rules
* **Exact-Match Target Keywords:** 40% (e.g. *"dental implant cost in Bangalore"*)
* **Partial-Match / Semantic Long-Tail:** 45% (e.g. *"read our in-depth walkthrough on the dental implant procedure timeline"*)
* **Branded / Navigational:** 15% (e.g. *"BrightSmile Dental Clinic's Bangalore implant guide"*)
* **NO Generic Anchors:** Strictly banned: *"click here"*, *"read more"*, *"this link"*, *"learn more"*. Every link must pass topical semantic context to Google crawlers.
