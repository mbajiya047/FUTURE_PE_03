## 🎯 Searcher Persona Profiles

### Persona A: "The Working Executive" (Age 32–48)
* **Location:** Indiranagar / Koramangala / Whitefield tech corridors.
* **Situation:** Lost a premolar or front tooth due to trauma or cracked root canal; time-poor; demands premium aesthetics and immediate function.
* **Key Objections:** "How many clinic visits does this take?", "Will anyone notice a missing tooth during the healing months?", "Can I work the next day?"
* **Primary Gateway:** Supporting Blog #2 (Procedure Timeline) & Supporting Blog #1 (Cost).

### Persona B: "The Health-Conscious Senior / Parent" (Age 55–72)
* **Location:** Jayanagar / Malleshwaram / Sadashivanagar.
* **Situation:** Multiple missing teeth or uncomfortable removable partial dentures causing chewing difficulties and nutritional compromises.
* **Key Objections:** "Is it safe at my age?", "I have Type 2 Diabetes / hypertension—can I still get implants?", "Is general anesthesia required?"
* **Primary Gateway:** Supporting Blog #3 (Safety & Candidacy) & Pillar Guide.

### Persona C: "The NRI / Medical Traveler" (Age 30–60)
* **Location:** US, UK, UAE, Australia visiting family in Bangalore for 2–4 weeks.
* **Situation:** Needs full-arch or multi-unit implants at high international standards but at Bangalore’s 70% cost advantage.
* **Key Objections:** "Can the surgical phase be completed within my 3-week vacation window?", "What warranty is provided on implant fixtures?"
* **Primary Gateway:** Supporting Blog #1 (Cost Comparison) & Pillar Guide.

## 🔗 Semantic Equity & PageRank Flow Strategy
1. **Pillar as Topical Anchor:** The Pillar Blog receives direct backlinks from homepage features, clinic service landing pages, and Google Business Profile links.
2. **Hub-and-Spoke Distribution:** The Pillar Page distributes PageRank downward to Supporting Articles #1 through #4 within contextual paragraphs.
3. **Upward Authority Recycling:** Each supporting article redirects 100% of reader attention toward either the Pillar Guide for holistic overview or the consultation scheduling funnel.
4. **Zero Dead Ends:** Every sub-article provides contextual navigation to sibling topics to minimize bounce rates and elevate average session duration above 4.5 minutes.
